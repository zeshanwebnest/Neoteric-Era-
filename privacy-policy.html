Privacy Policy

What we collect through this website, why, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Last updated: 26 July 2026

1. Who we are

Neoteric ERA ("we", "us") is a digital agency providing website development, search engine optimisation, advertising management and digital marketing services. Our office is at Office #105, 2nd Floor, Chuadhry Center, Multan Rd, Sodiwal, Lahore 54000, Pakistan.

For questions about this policy or about data we hold, contact info@neotericera.com or call +92 309 0155045.

2. What we collect

We collect two categories of information through this website.

Information you provide

When you complete the consultation form we collect the fields you fill in: name, work email, phone number, company, website address, the service you are interested in, an approximate budget range, a preferred start date, your project description and the state of the consent checkbox.

When you subscribe to the newsletter we collect your email address and the date you subscribed.

Information collected automatically

Standard analytics data: pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser type, referring source, and interactions such as form submissions. This is collected through Google Analytics 4.

What we do not collect We do not knowingly collect special category data (health, biometric, political or religious information), payment card details through this website, or data from anyone under 16.

4. What we do not do with it

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not share enquiry data with third parties for their own marketing.
  • We do not add enquiry email addresses to the newsletter without a separate opt-in.
  • We do not use enquiry content to train machine learning models.
  • We do not send unsolicited marketing to purchased or scraped lists.

6. Who we share it with

We use third-party service providers to operate the website and our business. Each processes data on our instructions.

  • Hosting provider — stores website files and logs. Name provider
  • Google (Analytics, Ads, Search Console) — measurement and advertising.
  • Email platform — newsletter delivery. Name provider
  • Form or CRM provider — receiving and storing enquiries. Name provider

We will also disclose data where required by law, or to establish or defend legal claims.

7. International transfers

We are based in Pakistan and our service providers operate in several jurisdictions, including the United States and the European Union. Where personal data is transferred internationally, an appropriate transfer mechanism must be in place.

The specific mechanisms relied upon must be confirmed by counsel. Legal review required

8. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become clients — 24 months, then deleted.
  • Client records — for the duration of the engagement plus the period required for tax and legal purposes.
  • Newsletter subscriptions — until you unsubscribe.
  • Analytics data — per the retention setting configured in Google Analytics.

Retention periods must reflect actual practice and applicable statutory requirements. Confirm

9. Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have your data deleted
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior processing
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data (we do not sell data)
  • Complain to your data protection authority

To exercise any of these, email info@neotericera.com. We will respond within the period required by the applicable law, and will not charge you or treat you differently for making a request.

10. Security

We use HTTPS across the site, restrict access to enquiry data to the people who need it, use multi-factor authentication on administrative accounts, and revoke access at the end of an engagement.

No system is completely secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and the relevant authority within the required timeframe.

11. Children

This website is intended for business audiences. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices or the law changes. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the current version. Material changes will be communicated to newsletter subscribers by email.
