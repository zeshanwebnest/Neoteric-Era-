Construction

The balance is knocked out of the shield with fill-rule="evenodd", not drawn on top of it. So the holes are genuinely transparent and one file works on white, on ink, or over a photograph.

That also fixes the previous mark, which drew thin strokes across seven separate paths and read as specks at header size. The thinnest element here is 6 grid units: 3.7px at 44 tall, 2.0px at 24.

Colours are the site's own grad-brand token and its slate-900 / slate-500 text colours. Nothing new is introduced.
