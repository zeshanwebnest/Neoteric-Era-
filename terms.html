Terms of Service

How engagements work: scope, payment, who owns what, and what happens when things go wrong or come to an end.

Last updated: 26 July 2026

1. About these terms

These terms apply to your use of this website and describe the standard basis on which Neoteric ERA provides services. Every engagement is also governed by a signed agreement setting out the specific scope, price and timeline; where the two conflict, the signed agreement takes precedence.

2. Services and scope

We provide website development, search engine optimisation, advertising management and digital marketing services. What is included in your engagement is defined in the written proposal, which becomes part of the agreement once accepted.

  • Project work is quoted as a fixed scope with a fixed price after a discovery call.
  • Ongoing services are provided as monthly retainers with a defined deliverable set.
  • Work outside the agreed scope is quoted separately as a change request, with its price and timeline impact stated before it begins.

3. What each side is responsible for

Responsibilities of each party during an engagement
We are responsible for You are responsible for
Delivering the agreed scope to the agreed standards Providing content, assets and approvals to the agreed schedule
A named point of contact and a weekly written update A decision-maker available for scheduled reviews
Testing across supported browsers and devices Timely access to hosting, analytics and third-party accounts
Flagging risks and delays as soon as they are known Ensuring you have rights to any content or assets you supply

Delays caused by content, approvals or third-party access move the delivery date by a corresponding period. We will tell you when that happens rather than absorb it silently and miss the date.

4. Fees and payment

  • Project fees are invoiced across milestones, beginning with a deposit before work starts.
  • Retainers are invoiced monthly in advance.
  • Invoices are issued in US dollars and payable by international transfer.
  • Advertising spend is paid by you directly to the platform. We take no percentage of media spend.
  • Third-party costs — licences, stock assets, hosting — are either paid by you directly or passed through at cost with your prior approval.

Payment terms, late payment provisions and any suspension of work for non-payment are set out in the engagement agreement. Confirm terms

5. Intellectual property

On full payment of the fees for the relevant phase, ownership of the deliverables created specifically for you transfers to you. That includes custom code, designs and written content produced under the engagement.

Two things sit outside that transfer:

  • Pre-existing materials and tooling we bring to the project remain ours. You receive a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use them as part of the delivered work.
  • Third-party components — open-source libraries, licensed themes, stock imagery, fonts — remain subject to their own licences, which we will identify.

Your domain, hosting, analytics and advertising accounts are registered in your name from the outset. We do not hold them as leverage.

6. Portfolio and publicity

We will not publish your name, logo, project imagery or results without your written permission. If you would prefer the engagement remains entirely confidential, say so and it will.

Where permission is given, we will show you the case study before publication and will remove it on request at any later date.

7. Confidentiality

Each side agrees to keep the other's non-public information confidential and to use it only for the engagement. We will sign your own non-disclosure agreement if you prefer.

Access to your systems is limited to the people who need it and revoked when the engagement ends.

8. Warranties and what we do not guarantee

We warrant that we will perform the services with reasonable skill and care, and that the deliverables will materially conform to the agreed scope.

We expressly do not guarantee:

  • Specific search rankings, traffic volumes or positions
  • Specific revenue, lead volume or conversion outcomes
  • Uninterrupted availability of third-party platforms we do not control
  • That search engine or advertising platform algorithm changes will not affect results

Anyone in this industry guaranteeing the first two is either misunderstanding the mechanics or misrepresenting them.

9. Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost profits, lost revenue or lost data, however arising.

Our total aggregate liability under an engagement is limited to the fees paid by you under that engagement in the twelve months preceding the claim.

Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited, including for fraud or for death or personal injury caused by negligence. Legal review required

10. Ending an engagement

  • Retainers may be ended by either side with the notice period stated in the agreement, typically 30 days.
  • Projects may be ended for convenience, with fees payable for work completed and committed to that point.
  • Either side may end the agreement immediately for material breach that is not remedied within a reasonable period after written notice.

On termination we will:

  • Transfer credentials, code repositories and design files
  • Provide a written summary of work in progress and its state
  • Remove our access to your systems
  • Retain records only as required for tax and legal purposes

A clean exit is a reasonable thing to want and we do not make it difficult.

11. Use of this website

Content on this website is provided for general information. It does not constitute professional advice and should not be relied upon as the sole basis for a business decision.

The Neoteric ERA name, logo and site content are our property or used under licence. You may not copy or reproduce them without permission, other than normal browsing and fair quotation with attribution.

12. Governing law

The governing law and jurisdiction applicable to an engagement are stated in the engagement agreement. Legal review required

13. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as our practices or the law changes. The "last updated" date reflects the current version. Changes do not alter the terms of a signed engagement agreement already in force.
